If you're in Tokyo right now, drop whatever you're doing on your next free evening and head to Shinjuku. The Tokyo Metropolitan Government Building — that iconic twin-tower landmark in West Shinjuku — has been transformed into a massive canvas for one of the most spectacular free events in the city: **Pokémon Card Game TOKYO LUMINOUS NIGHT**.

This isn't just another light show. It's a celebration of the Pokémon Trading Card Game's 30th anniversary, and it turns the entire facade of one of Tokyo's most recognizable buildings into a living, breathing Pokémon card battle. Think skyscraper-sized Pikachu, oversized holographic cards projected across 48 floors of glass and steel, and a soundtrack that makes the whole plaza feel like you've stepped inside a real Pokémon arena.

## What Is TOKYO LUMINOUS NIGHT?

TOKYO LUMINOUS NIGHT is Tokyo's ongoing projection mapping project at the Metropolitan Government Building. The city regularly commissions new works from different collaborators, and the latest addition — launched on March 20, 2026 — is a collaboration with The Pokémon Company to celebrate 30 years of the Pokémon Trading Card Game.

The Pokémon TCG first launched in Japan in October 1996 and has since become a worldwide phenomenon, with cards sold in 84 countries and regions. This projection mapping piece brings the distinctive artwork of Pokémon cards to life in three dimensions, using the building's unique architecture — its recessed windows and vertical columns — to create a forced-perspective illusion where Pokémon appear to leap out of the building itself.

The premiere on March 20 drew approximately 8,000 spectators to the Citizens' Plaza, including families, international tourists, and dedicated Pokémon card collectors.

## Schedule & Screening Times

The show runs on **Saturdays, Sundays, and national holidays** with three screenings per day:

- **6:30 PM**
- **7:30 PM**
- **9:00 PM**

Note: Screening times may shift as sunset times change through the seasons. There is currently no announced end date, so the show is expected to continue for the foreseeable future.

The show runs even in light rain, though heavy weather may cause cancellations.

## How to Get There

The viewing area is **Citizens' Plaza (Tomin Hiroba)**, the open plaza directly in front of the Tokyo Metropolitan Government Building No. 1.

**Access:**
- **JR Shinjuku Station** (West Exit) — 10-minute walk
- **Tokyo Metro Marunouchi Line** Nishi-Shinjuku Station — 5-minute walk
- **Toei Oedo Line** Tochomae Station — directly connected

If you're staying anywhere along the JR Yamanote Line or near Shinjuku, this is an easy evening addition to your itinerary.

## Tips for Visitors

**Arrive early.** The plaza can get crowded, especially for the first screening (6:30 PM). Arriving 15–20 minutes before showtime will give you a better viewing spot.

**Best viewing position.** Stand near the center of Citizens' Plaza for the full effect. The projection is calibrated for a specific viewing angle, and the 3D illusions work best from the front-center.

**Combine it with dinner.** West Shinjuku has excellent dining options. The Omoide Yokocho (Memory Lane) alley near the station is perfect for yakitori and beer before or after the show. Alternatively, the Tokyo Metropolitan Government Building's observation deck (free admission, open until 10 PM) offers stunning night views — pair it with the show for a full evening.

**Bring a jacket.** Even in spring, evenings in Tokyo can be cool, and you'll be standing outside for at least 15–20 minutes.
